A substitute for every ingredient

Flour, meat, cheese, sauce, sweetener — in Fiteo you can swap any ingredient in any recipe. Pick what you’re after (or describe it in your own words) and Fiteo suggests substitutes that fit that particular dish, then recalculates the calories and macros straight away.

Five goals — or your own words

Fewer caloriesE.g. sour cream → 0% Greek yogurt, mayonnaise → yogurt with mustard, bacon → turkey ham.
More proteinE.g. plain yogurt → skyr, regular pasta → lentil pasta, cream cheese on toast → cottage cheese.
Lactose-freeE.g. milk → lactose-free milk or oat drink, cottage cheese → lactose-free cottage cheese, butter → ghee.
VegetarianE.g. ground beef → lentils, chicken → chickpeas or tofu, meat stock → vegetable stock, gelatine → agar.
CheaperE.g. walnuts → sunflower seeds, fresh raspberries → frozen, Parmesan → a mature hard cheese.
Your own wordsGot another idea? Write what you want — e.g. “no sugar, but still sweet”.

These are just examples. You get a substitute for every ingredient — Fiteo takes the rest of the recipe into account, so it suggests something different in a cake than in a soup.

FAQ

Can I swap any ingredient?

Yes. You can get a substitute for any ingredient in any recipe in Fiteo — including recipes saved from Instagram or TikTok.

Which goals can I choose?

Five ready-made ones: fewer calories, more protein, lactose-free, vegetarian and cheaper. You can also describe what you want in your own words, and if the suggestions don’t fit, pick another goal.

Do substitutes always have fewer calories?

No. With “more protein”, “lactose-free”, “vegetarian” or “cheaper” the calories may stay similar. Fiteo shows how the calories and macros of the whole serving change.

How much does it cost?

Your first substitute is free. Unlimited substitutes are part of Fiteo Pro.
