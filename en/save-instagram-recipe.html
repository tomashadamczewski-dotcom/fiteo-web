How to save a recipe from Instagram — and count its calories

You see a great recipe in a reel, you save it… and never go back. Even if you do, you have no idea how many calories it has. Here's how to save a recipe from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube with the ingredients, steps and calories in one place.

The no-app way (and why it falls short)

Instagram lets you save a post: tap the bookmark under the reel → Save, then find it in your profile → Saved. It works, but:

  • the recipe stays in the caption — while cooking you keep scrolling the reel;
  • saved posts get lost among everything else;
  • you still don't know the calories per serving.

With Fiteo: two ways, one result

Option 1

Share the reel to Fiteo

Under the reel on Instagram: Share → Fiteo. No copying, straight from Instagram.

Option 2

Copy and paste the link

Under the reel: Share → Copy link, then in Fiteo: Recipes → Import from link and paste it.

Both work on iPhone and Android — from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. The result: ingredients with amounts, the steps and the recipe's parts (e.g. dough, cream, crumble), plus calories and macros per serving.

What you can do next

  • Make it yours. On an ingredient, tap Suggest a substitute: lighter, higher-protein, lactose-free, vegetarian, cheaper or in your own words — Fiteo recalculates the recipe.
  • Eat and log it. One tap adds a serving to your diary and your daily balance updates.
  • Share it. Send the recipe as a link — friends can open it even without the app.
