How to save a recipe from Instagram — and count its calories

You see a great recipe in a reel, you save it… and never go back. Even if you do, you have no idea how many calories it has. Here's how to save a recipe from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube with the ingredients, steps and calories in one place.

The no-app way (and why it falls short)

Instagram lets you save a post: tap the bookmark under the reel → Save, then find it in your profile → Saved. It works, but:

  • the recipe stays in the caption — while cooking you keep scrolling the reel;
  • saved posts get lost among everything else;
  • you still don't know the calories per serving.

With Fiteo: recipe, steps and calories in 3 steps

  1. Copy the reel's link. On Instagram: Share → Copy link. The same works on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook.
  2. Paste it into Fiteo. Recipes → Import from link, then paste the address.
  3. Done. Fiteo pulls out the ingredients with amounts, the steps and the recipe's parts (e.g. dough, cream, crumble), and counts calories and macros per serving.

What you can do next

  • Make it yours. On an ingredient, tap Suggest a substitute: higher-protein, lighter, lactose-free or vegetarian — Fiteo recalculates the recipe.
  • Eat and log it. One tap adds a serving to your diary and your daily balance updates.
  • Share it. Send the recipe as a link — friends can open it even without the app.

Where Fiteo gets the recipe from

We read the caption under the reel first — that's where creators usually put the amounts, so it's the most accurate source. If the caption has no recipe, Fiteo reconstructs it from the video itself; amounts can then be approximate, so check them before saving — calories depend on them.

Calories are counted from the ingredient amounts. We tested how well that works on 100 dishes — accuracy test results (in Polish).

FAQ

Can I save a recipe from TikTok or YouTube?

Yes. Fiteo imports recipes from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook links.

What if the creator didn't write the recipe in the caption?

Fiteo reconstructs it from the video. Amounts may be approximate — check them before saving.

Does Fiteo download the video to my phone?

No. Only the recipe goes into the app: the title, ingredients, steps and nutrition.

Will the creator know?

No. Importing is like saving a recipe for yourself. If you liked it, leave the creator a like or a comment.

How do I count calories for a recipe I already have?

You can also add a recipe by hand in Fiteo: type the ingredients with amounts and the app counts calories and macros per serving.
