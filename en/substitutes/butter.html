Butter substitute — lighter, but wisely

Butter has over 700 kcal per 100 g. In many recipes you can use less of it or swap it for something lighter — but not everywhere, because fat carries flavour and structure.

Where and what to swap

  • In cakes, muffins and brownies: replace half the butter with applesauce or yogurt (1:1 by volume). The bake will be moister and lighter; swapping all of it can make it rubbery.
  • On bread: cottage cheese, hummus or mashed avocado — more flavour and usually fewer calories than a layer of butter.
  • For frying: olive oil and other oils have as many calories as butter (even more), but it’s easier to use less — measure a teaspoon instead of pouring.
  • In shortcrust pastry: here butter is hard to replace without losing the crumbly texture. Better to make a smaller portion than to swap the ingredient.

FAQ

Is olive oil lighter than butter?

No — olive oil has about 884 kcal per 100 g, butter about 717 kcal. Olive oil does have a better fat profile.

What can I use instead of butter in a cake?

In a sponge or muffin batter — replace part of it with applesauce or yogurt. In shortcrust pastry — butter is best, just less of it.
